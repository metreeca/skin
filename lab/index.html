Design system

Essential design tokens and styles

A typed token contract and the stylesheet giving it values. Every sample on this page is painted by the tokens themselves, so what you see is what an app gets by importing @metreeca/skin/index.css and nothing else.

    Click any token to copy its var() reference. Colour samples follow the scheme on show, except the anchors, split between the light scheme on the left and the dark one on the right.

    Foundations

    Colours

    Eight anchors carry literal values; every other colour derives from them, so retuning an anchor carries the whole interface along.

    Anchors

    The page pair, the brand accents and the four steps of the meaning scale.

    Meaning scale

    Every meaning a widget carries lands on one of four steps. A notice is marked by the step, tinted by its fill and titled in its stroke; its body keeps the page ink.

    Text and marks

    Live contrast against the page and the table stripe, in the scheme on show. Text holds 4.5:1, marks and the focus ring 3:1; disabled and placeholder ink are deliberately below budget.

    Fills

    Grounds for editing, striping and interaction states, and the tints of the meaning scale.

    Foundations

    Palettes

    Colours a chart, a coding or a map reads. They say where a value sits or which thing it belongs to, and never stand in for a role.

    Scales

    Ten steps each, numbered by the share of the anchor they carry. Gray, subtle and strong follow the anchors; heat codes bands by hue and always needs a legend in text.

    Series

    Nine slots telling one thing from another, ordered so neighbours stay apart under colour vision deficiency. On scatters and maps, where every pair is compared, only the first three hold.

    Areas

    Four classes filling shapes, as two light and dark pairs. A map keeps its boundaries drawn and names its classes in a legend; a fifth class is a second map.

    Markup

    Alerts

    GitHub alerts, as Markdown renders > [!NOTE] and its siblings: notes on the subtle accent, every other kind on a step of the meaning scale.

    Note

    Useful information a reader should know, even when skimming, marked by the subtle accent.

    Important

    Key information a reader needs to succeed.

    Tip

    Advice for doing things better or more easily.

    Warning

    Urgent information that needs immediate attention to avoid problems.

    Caution

    Advice about the risks or negative outcomes of certain actions.
