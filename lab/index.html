Design system

Essential design tokens and styles

A typed token contract and the stylesheet giving it values. Every sample on this page is painted by the tokens themselves, so what you see is what an app gets by importing @metreeca/skin/presets/roboto.css and nothing else.

    Foundations

    Colours

    A handful of anchors carry literal values; every other colour derives from them, so retuning an anchor carries the whole interface along.

    Anchors

    The page pair, the brand accents and the focus mark.

    Meaning scale

    Every meaning a widget carries lands on one of five steps, each with its own anchor, stroke and tint. A notice is marked by the step and titled in its stroke; its body keeps the page ink.

    Text and marks

    Live contrast against the page and the table stripe, in the scheme on show. Text holds 4.5:1, marks and the focus ring 3:1; disabled and placeholder ink are deliberately below budget.

    Fills

    Grounds for editing, striping and interaction states.

    Foundations

    Palettes

    Colours an author picks, and a chart, a coding or a map reads. They say how content was coloured, where a value sits or which thing it belongs to, and never stand in for a role.

    Editor

    Colours an author picks for content, as text or fill: a neutral and five hues by three steps, strong standing out from the page and subtle staying close to it. Their pairing is the author's.

    Scales

    Ten steps each, numbered by the share of the anchor they carry. Gray, subtle and strong follow the anchors; heat codes bands by hue and always needs a legend in text.

    Series

    Nine slots telling one thing from another, ordered so neighbours stay apart under colour vision deficiency. On scatters and maps, where every pair is compared, only the first three hold.

    Areas

    Four classes filling shapes, as two light and dark pairs. A map keeps its boundaries drawn and names its classes in a legend; a fifth class is a second map.

    Markup

    Alerts

    Markdown alerts, as rendered from > [!NOTE] and its siblings, each kind on a step of the meaning scale.

    Note

    Useful information that users should know, even when skimming content.

    Tip

    Helpful advice for doing things better or more easily.

    Important

    Key information users need to know to achieve their goal.

    Warning

    Urgent info that needs immediate user attention to avoid problems.

    Caution

    Advises about risks or negative outcomes of certain actions.
